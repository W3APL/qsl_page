## Weather

We are definitely getting into fall weather. Today the weather will be amazing, sun shining and high in the 70s. It will be a bit on the chillier side in the mornings the rest of the weekend, but highs in the mid 60s, and lows down in the high 30s. Very low chances of rain though, so dress warmly while you are out taking your radio for a walk.

The solar conditions show an SFI of 89 (solar flux index (SFI) is the intensity of solar radio emissions. Higher the better up to about 300, so not great, and a significant drop from 106 last week), A index of 4 ( daily numerical average of the K Index of Earth's geomagnetic field and activity. The lower the better from 0-400, and a drop from 20 last week), and K index of 1 (lower is better, 1= VERY quiet and stable Earths magnetic field, a drop from 2 last week). 10-17m are poor, with the rest of the bands being fair to good. This weekend is going to be a bit trying when it comes to long distance communications, but get out there and give it a try!
