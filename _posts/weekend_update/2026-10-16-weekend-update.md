## Weather

You might not have noticed the low amount of precipitation this morning, I know it has my spirits up. Thankfully, right at about the time we are getting off work today, there is supposed to be a Thunderstorm…. So today is a bit of a wash. (get it!!?!?!?)_ However, Saturday and Sunday should be much nicer, although on the chilly side, with lows in the 30’s and highs in the mid to high 50’s. Fall is definitely here!!

The solar conditions show an SFI of 126 (solar flux index (SFI) is the intensity of solar radio emissions. Higher the better up to about 300, so not great, and a rise from 119 last week). The A index is 9 ( daily numerical average of the K Index of Earth's geomagnetic field and activity. The lower the better from 0-400, and the same as last week), and K index of 2 (lower is better, 2= quiet and stable Earths magnetic field, a drop from 4 last week). 10-12m are poor in the night only, with the rest of the bands being fair to good.

- October 16th–18th 2026 – Third Full Weekend of October – [Autumn Support Your Local Parks](https://docs.pota.app/docs/events.html#support-your-parks) – Parks on the Air (POTA). Make any contact, or activate any park during this weekend for the Autumn Activator/Hunter for Support Your Local Parks.
- October 16th-18th, 2026, National Scouting America (formerly Boy Scouts) (K2BSA) – [Jamboree on the Air (JOTA)](https://www.scouting.org/international/jota-joti/jota/) – the world’s largest Scouting event. The three-day event using amateur radio and the internet to connect Scouts worldwide for a full weekend of on-air and online activities that promote friendship and global citizenship. Many Scouting America Troops, Packs and Venturing crews from around the world, and specifically in Maryland will hold events at local parks and Scouting Reservations.
  - Frequencies that will be monitored by the K2BSA (BSA Amateur Radio Club) can be found at <https://k2bsa.net/scout-frequencies/>
  - KC3FOX will be at a Scout Reservation (trying to stay warm) and will be monitoring Echolink (JOTA365 channel), 20 meters (probably around 14.290 mhz), 6m (50.160 mhz), 2m (146.520 mhz), and maybe have APRS up and running (basically to advertise our frequencies). It should be a blast!
- October 17th, 2026 – St Mary’s County Amateur Radio Association is hosting its Annual Tailgate Festival. 24801 Three Notch Rd. Hollywood, MD 20636. (1hr 30 mins from APL). More information at <https://smcara.org/2026/09/12/tailgate-2026/>.
