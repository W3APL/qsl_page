## Weather

The weather for this weekend should be mostly nice, with cloudy to partly cloudy throughout the weekend, leading into showers on Sunday evening. Highs are in the low 80’s, and lows in the low 60’s. Man, this is great camping weather…IDEA!!!!

The solar conditions show an SFI of 97 (solar flux index (SFI) is the intensity of solar radio emissions. Higher the better up to about 300, so not great, and a drop from 115 last week, and has been dropping steadily), A index of 9 ( daily numerical average of the K Index of Earth's geomagnetic field and activity. The lower the better from 0-400, and a the same from last week), and K index of 0 (lower is better, 0=  VERY quiet and stable Earths magnetic field, a drop from 2 last week). 10-12m are poor, with the rest of the bands being fair to good.
