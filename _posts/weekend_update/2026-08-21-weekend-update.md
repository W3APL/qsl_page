## Weather

Mostly sunny (as opposed to partly cloudy?) with a 65% chance of Rain on Saturday. Highs in the high 70s to low 80s throughout the weekend and lows in the mid 60s. The solar conditions show an SFI of 125 (Higher the better up to about 300, so not great, and a raise from 108 last week), A index of 11 (lower is better from 0-400, and a raise from 7 last week), and K index of 1 (lower better, 1 = quiet Earths magnetic field, down from 2 last week). 10 and 12m are fair to poor, with 15m through 80m should be pretty good.
