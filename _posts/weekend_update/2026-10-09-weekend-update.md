Drum Roll, Please!!It is time for all the news that is the news for your Weekend Update, on everything that will keep your radio humming, and your antennas tuned.

## Weather

The weekend weather should be somewhat great for Radio Activities, and with fall approaching make sure that you check your antennas (if they are outside) and think about weather sealing them again. However, today should be beautiful, partly cloudy with high in the mid 70s, and low around 51°F (noticed that this morning!!). Saturday should be about the same, with a small chance of rain in the evening forecast. Sunday, you might need to build a boat, cause it is going to be a downpour, and temps in the mid to low 60s.

The solar conditions show:

- SFI of 119 and a huge jump from 92 last week (solar flux index (SFI) is the intensity of solar radio emissions. Higher the better up to about 300, so not great)
- A index of 6, a rise from 4 last week (daily numerical average of the K Index of Earth's geomagnetic field and activity. The lower the better from 0-400)
- K index of 4 (lower is better, 4= unsettled or active Earths magnetic field, a rise from 0 last week)
- The 10m-12m bands are poor, with the rest of the bands being fair.
