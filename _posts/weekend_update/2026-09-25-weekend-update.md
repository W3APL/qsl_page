## Weather

Some minor news about a Nor’easter hitting New Jersey and the surrounding states aside, the weather today will be nice at a high of 74°F to a low of 57°F, and the temperature dropping throughout the weekend to a high in the mid 60s. Weather should start getting distinctly wetter towards Saturday with showers expected from Saturday evening throughout Sunday. So if you are going to do some outdoors fun, do it tonight or early tomorrow.

The solar conditions show:

- An SFI of 112 (solar flux index (SFI) is the intensity of solar radio emissions. Higher the better up to about 300, so not great, and a raise from 97 last week)
- A index of 16 ( daily numerical average of the K Index of Earth's geomagnetic field and activity. The lower the better from 0-400, and a rise from 9 last week)
- K index of 3 (lower is better, 3= unsettled and weak Earths magnetic field activity, a rise from 1 last week).
- 10-12m are poor, with the rest of the bands being fair to good, with the exception of 40m-80m during the day.
